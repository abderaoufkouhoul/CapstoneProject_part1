# CapstoneProject_part1

Neural Networks applied to the placement of digital cells


Adoption of ML/AI algorithm in ASIC design is becoming a hot topic. Hence, electronic design automation (EDA) tools providers are increasingly integrating ML/AI algorithms in new generation of their tools.

The main driven motivations for exploring the use of ML/AI algorithm in the domain of Application Specific Integrated Circuits ( ASIC) design are:

    The run-time is considerably high even with powerful servers and cloud machines. This due to the high complexity and big size of advanced ASIC design. Therefore, the expectation is high when it comes to the adoption of ML/AI to reduce run-time and complexity of advanced designs.

    The area of ML/AI is evolving at high speed. Hence, taking a lead in highly competitive business.

    
Our focus is on the  use of Neural Networks in the field of the placement of digital cells. Throughout literature review, we came across important research works where the placement of digital cells was 
evaluated as a non-linear optimization problem. 
More details on the modelling of this problematic with Neural Network is provided in the file available on data/capstone_project.pdf .
